Simulation

Model assumptions

Three strategies use identical simulated customer arrivals: fixed price, competitor matching and inventory-aware adjustments.

Demand uses a Poisson arrival process and a logistic price-response curve. Stock is finite, with no replenishment. Prices stay within 65–140% of base price and above a 5% unit margin.

Results exclude taxes, returns, advertising and carrying costs. Remaining inventory is valued at cost. These are experiments under assumptions, not forecasts of actual revenue.
